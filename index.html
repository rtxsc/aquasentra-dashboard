NWQS Malaysia Framework & Marine Life Viability Guide

CLASS I / II Clean / Excellent

Water Supply: Practically no treatment to conventional treatment required.
Marine Life: Optimal conditions for sensitive aquatic species (Fishery I & II), highly suitable for prawn aquaculture.
Recreation: Safe for body contact.

CLASS III Moderate

Water Supply: Extensive treatment required. Safe for livestock drinking.
Marine Life: Suitable for common, tolerant species (Fishery III). Induces moderate stress for sensitive marine life, which may reduce prawn growth rates.

CLASS IV / V Polluted / Critical

Water Supply: Unsuitable for drinking. Restricted to irrigation (Class IV) or totally unusable (Class V).
Marine Life: High stress to critical danger. Severe deviations causing poor moulting or immediate aquatic mortality.
